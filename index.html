The math — no hidden steps
FREE CREDITS ARE NOT COINS

Every new user gets 1,000 FUSI usage credits to use the ecosystem. Credits are spent on operations; they are not coins, they are not minted, and they are not counted in the minted supply. Two separate books.

HOLDS — THE 7-DAY VESTING LAW (LEDGER h=195)

An earn grant is granted on the chain at once, and its coin exists from that moment. Earned FUSI vests over 7 days: the grant's coins release daily across the window, in the order they were earned, and the entirety is released on day 7 — a coin can move only at or after its own release moment. Fully the holder's on day 7.

Book value and rarity value are two separate labeled numbers, always — never merged into one figure.

Status vocabulary — the tool's exact words
  • UNVERIFIED — the evidence does not prove the unit: an L1-only grant, or a player-submitted score_claim, which grants nothing and is never spendable.
  • VERIFIED — L2-VERIFIED replay evidence rides the unit itself.
  • VESTING — granted or paid, and the effective hold_until is still ahead.
  • VESTED — hold_until reached; the spendable moment has passed.
  • REJECTED — revoked by a later block, or the grant's status is not “granted”.
  • SETTLEMENT UNKNOWN — an intent or accept exists with no settle; the coin's movement is uncertain and never spendable on that evidence.

STEP 5

Trade on a declared market.

The marketplace is declared on-chain — market_registry, block h=192 — and TRANSFERS OPEN was declared at block h=169, so the path a sale settles through is already live.

Listings are intents — they never move a coin

A listing is a public intent posted by a coin's owner. Listing, delisting, and browsing move nothing: a coin changes hands only when a sale settles through the transfer path — the same consent path and verification evidence as any transfer.

Every listing shows three labeled numbers, and they are never merged into one:

Book value

What the ledger granted the coin at mint — always labeled BOOK.

Rarity value

Book value carried through the coin's rarity signature — always labeled RARITY.

Ask

The price the seller sets for this listing — always labeled ASK, separate from both values.

Sale fee

5% of the ask (PROVISIONAL, min 1 FUSI) goes to the FEE POOL. Gifts move fee-free.

Open listings

List a coin

Draft listing email Fill every field to arm the draft.

Listing drafts an email to the market inbox. Sending it asks the machine to append a market_list block — a public intent, never a sale. Only the coin's current owner can list; the machine verifies ownership, grant holds, and in-flight locks first.

Delist

Draft delist email Delisting is allowed any time before settlement.

A delist appends a market_delist block citing the listing. Only the lister can delist, and only while the listing is still open — once a sale settles, the listing is fulfilled and cannot be withdrawn.

How a sale happens — five steps, all on the chain
STEP 1
LIST

The owner appends a market_list block: coin, ask, expiry. The coin does not move.

STEP 2
ACCEPT

A buyer's accept email materializes a transfer intent at the listing's exact terms, and the accept stands on-chain.

STEP 3
SETTLE

The transfer path settles: gate evidence checked, holds and wash limits enforced, the coin changes hands.

STEP 4
FEE

The fee is cut from the sale price and recorded as a pool_event citing the transfer.

STEP 5
POOL

The fee lands in the FEE POOL, public on the ledger, feeding the platform's pools.

The rules, in plain language
  • New grants vest over 7 days. Under the hold law (block h=195), a grant's coins release daily across the 7-day window, in grant order, and the entirety is released on day 7 — a coin can move only at or after its own release moment.
  • Transfer fees feed the FEE POOL. When coins move, the 5% fee (min 1 FUSI) goes to the FEE POOL (declared h=189) — separate from the game pool, and never to a person.
  • Transfers are open. Declared at block h=169: transfers settle through the consent path with verification evidence, a 5% fee (min 1 FUSI) to the FEE POOL — and gifts move free.
  • FUSI is a usage credit — no dollar price, ever. FUSI pays for play and tools inside this platform; it is not money and is never priced in dollars.
