CONTROLS (DECLARED)

  • ← / hold left side of the screen — steer left
  • → / hold right side of the screen — steer right
  • ↑ or SPACE / FLARE button — flare: slows the fall to a crawl for 24 ticks, then cools down for 150 ticks. One code per tick; a flare press wins over steering that tick.
  • No input — glide straight; your sideways speed fades one step per tick.

One hit ends the run: touch a rooftop edge, crane, scaffold pole, billboard or a bird flock and the canopy collapses. Thread the open channel in every row, dive through gold rings (+25), pass obstacles (+10 near, +4 mid ledges), and every 500 of altitude pays a checkpoint (+5). Nothing pays for falling by itself.
