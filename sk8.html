HOW TO PLAY SK8

The game PAUSES while this is open. Press GOT IT (or PAUSE again) to resume exactly where you left off — no ticks lost, none skipped.

  1. Buttons first — OLLIE (bottom left), FLIP (bottom right), GRIND (bottom right, hold). These big gold buttons are the whole game. OLLIE jumps. FLIP spins the board mid-air. GRIND only counts while you are HOLDING it — lift your thumb and the grind stops on the very next tick. Nothing is latched.
  2. Gestures still work as alternates. TAP the street = ollie. DOUBLE-TAP or SWIPE UP mid-air = kickflip. HOLD your finger on a rail = grind. Keys: SPACE ollie, K / ArrowUp flip, G / ArrowDown grind.
  3. Ollie timing is the game. Jump just before a car and you sail over it (+20). Jump just before a gap and you fly across it (+30). Too early and you land on the car. Too late and you fall in the gap. Both are bails.
  4. Kickflip (+25) only works airborne. A kickflip inside an obstacle line — jump a car, flip, land it — grows your chain an extra link when you land. A flat kickflip scores but never grows the chain.
  5. Grind (+5 every tick, +15 clean exit). You must be holding GRIND when you reach the rail. Sparks fly while you grind. Let go any time to drop off.
  6. Gaps — be airborne when the far edge passes. Grounded over a gap and you fall in: bail. Cars work the same: grounded into a car, or landing on one, is a bail.
  7. Stairs — ollie as you ride over them for a stair-drop bonus (+15). Just riding down is safe, never a bail.
  8. Pause — press PAUSE (top right, bigger now) or P any time mid-run. The street freezes and this panel opens. GOT IT resumes.
  9. Chain = money-in-waiting (PROPOSED). Only obstacle-linked tricks grow the chain. Flat tricks score points but never grow it. A bail resets the chain to 0. Three bails ends the run.
