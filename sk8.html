HOW TO PLAY SK8

  1. Ollie — TAP the screen (or press SPACE). Jump. Time it so you are high over a car or across a gap.
  2. Kickflip — DOUBLE-TAP or SWIPE UP mid-air (or press K / ArrowUp). +25 while airborne. A kickflip inside an obstacle line also grows your chain when you land it.
  3. Grind — HOLD your finger on a rail (or hold G / ArrowDown). +5 every tick on the rail, +15 for a clean exit. Let go any time — but you must be holding when you reach the rail.
  4. Gaps — be airborne when the far edge passes. Grounded over a gap and you fall in: bail. Cars work the same: grounded into a car, or landing on one, is a bail.
  5. Stairs — ollie as you ride over them for a stair-drop bonus. Just riding down is safe, never a bail.
  6. Chain = money-in-waiting (PROPOSED). Only obstacle-linked tricks grow the chain. Flat tricks score points but never grow it. A bail resets the chain to 0. Three bails ends the run.
