HOW TO PLAY SK8

The game PAUSES while this is open. Press GOT IT (or PAUSE again) to resume exactly where you left off.

  • Buttons — OLLIE (bottom left), FLIP (bottom right), GRIND (bottom right, hold). OLLIE jumps. FLIP spins the board mid-air. GRIND only counts while you are HOLDING it — lift your thumb and the grind stops on the very next tick. Nothing is latched.
  • Keyboard — Space = ollie, F or K = flip, hold G = grind, P = pause.
  • Score — tricks x flow multiplier. Chain grows only on obstacle-linked tricks (car ollie, gap clear, stair ollie, grind exit, linked kickflip), up to x4. Flat ollies/flips score but never grow the chain. 3 bails ends the run; 600m finishes it.
  • Grind (+5 every tick, +15 clean exit). Hold GRIND when you reach a rail. Sparks fly while you grind.
  • Cars, gaps, stairs. Ollie the cars, clear the gaps while airborne, drop the stairs. Grounded into a car or a gap is a bail.
