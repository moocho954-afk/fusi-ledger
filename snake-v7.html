STREAK & THE BONUS ROUND (AS DECLARED, h=356)

Every diamond starts a 60-tick window. Eat the next diamond inside the window and the streak grows; let the window lapse and the streak resets. Chain 10 on-pace diamonds and the bonus triggers: the board freezes and you drop through a time pocket into the bonus corridor for 1,000 ticks.

In the corridor the snake’s head is enormous and strides forward through three lanes (centres −3 / 0 / +3, walls at |x| = 6). Diamonds stream at you — be covering their lane at the catch plane to collect. A perfect streak in the corridor escalates the lights, the combo counter and the head’s energy, and every corridor catch lands with a shake, a tilt and a buzz. But the corridor is sudden-death: one miss, or an outward steer at the wall lane (an edge), ends the bonus on that tick. Survive to zero on the countdown and you return to the frozen board exactly as it was left — with the streak spent (it resets to 0).

All numbers on this page — the streak, the window, the countdown, every catch and miss — come from the v7 core’s own state and events. The page only renders them.
